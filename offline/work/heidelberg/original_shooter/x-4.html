Important Information

Important information in the text is marked by symbols that are used as follows:

Warning: Contains information that must be observed to protect the operator from potential hazards or damage.

Caution: Contains information that must be observed to prevent damage to hardware or software.

Note: Contains important general or additional information on a specific topic.

Prerequisite: Lists prerequisites that must be met to allow the following steps to be performed.
