Storing Jobs

Note: You can only store jobs that are completed.

You can store jobs in a separate tab to keep your job list nice and clear. Stored jobs move to the "Depot" tab and you can no longer see them in the other tabs of the job lists. Stored jobs can be reprinted if required. They can be deleted permanently when they are no longer needed.

To store one or more jobs, mark them in the job list and right-click to display the context-sensitive menu. Select the "Depot" command.


The selected jobs are stored. You can view these jobs in the "Depot" tab, reprint or permanently delete them.

Automatic Storing of Jobs

In the virtual printer setup (see Opening a Virtual Printer or Creating a New One), you can set that jobs will be archived automatically.


When you check the "Transfer to The Depot After" option and set a time in the text box, all the jobs processed by this virtual printer will be stored automatically after this period.
