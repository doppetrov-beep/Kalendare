Prinect Tiff Toolbox

Prinect Tiff Toolbox is an "offline" tool that allows you to edit Tiff-B files independently of a RIP or a workflow system.

The Prinect Tiff Toolbox has the following functions:

  • Import of TIFF-B files to Prinect Shooter from third-party RIP/workflow systems.
  • Trimming, rotating and mirroring of Tiff-B files.
  • Descreening of Tiff-B files and creation of either a contone Tiff or contone PDF file.
  • Viewing of Tiff-B files in their original resolution in the Bitmap Viewer.
