Job list context-sensitive menu

Position the mouse pointer on an item in the job list and click with the right mouse button. A context-sensitive menu displays, showing some functions that you can open for processing your job. Whether or not an option is available depends on the context indicated by the mouse pointer when you call up the function and on what options are installed. You can perform the following actions:


  • Set the priority. The priority setting does not affect jobs whose processing is so far on that you can no longer change the processing order (see also Printing "Urgent" Jobs Next).
  • Create a new job
  • Open a job
  • Delete a completed or aborted job
  • Send job data to the output device
  • Store the job (see Storing Jobs)
  • Export Tiff-B jobs (see the Export of Tiff-B Files)
  • View statistics about processed jobs (see the Statistics about Processed Jobs)
  • Use the functions for starting, pausing, resuming and reprinting a marked job or for starting a reprint of single separations (see the Restarting, Continuing, Pausing, Canceling Your Jobs).
  • Expand or collapse all grouped jobs sent with the "File Based Device Information (FBDI)" protocol Use these commands to expand or collapse all the jobs marked by a folder icon at the same time.
  • Deselect single or several items in the job list.
  • Create a job report (see the Creating a Job Report).
