Monitoring

This tab shows you at a glance how long it is until maintenance is due. After maintenance, you can also tick the work as done and reset the maintenance interval to its initial value.

Component

This column lists the components that require maintenance.

The "Service Maintenance" row indicates whether or not maintenance is to be done by the service technicians. Please contact your maintenance service if maintenance is required by service technicians.

Status

A progress bar in this column indicates the state.

Red: Part of the maintenance interval expired.

Green: Part of the maintenance interval still available.

Next Maintenance

This column shows in units how much maintenance interval is still available.

Units can be hours, plates or punches.

The units Weeks and m2 are also shown in the "Service" and "Research and Development" modes.

Maintenance Completed

You can tick off an item of maintenance after it's completed in this column.

Apply

Click "Apply" to reset the maintenance interval of work that is ticked off to its initial value after you confirm a prompt. This button is only enabled if at least one item of maintenance is ticked as done.
