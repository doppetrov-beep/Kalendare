100 Label

You can mark the changes made to the devices in the 100 label in this tab. Changes can only be marked in the "Service" or "Research and Development" mode. These modification labels are also located in the devices. The markings on the devices must match those made at the GUI.

Apply

The settings are saved when you click "Apply".

Cancel

Your last inputs are undone by clicking "Cancel" unless the "Apply" button was clicked.

System upgrade

Reference key

The reference key is generated automatically by the recorder hardware and shown in this box.

Activation key

To be able to use the upgrade to the 4up permanently, you must request an activation key from your Heidelberg agency and enter it in this box.

Start Temporary Mode

The upgrade to a 4up device is enabled for a period of approx. 14 days when you click this button. During this period, you must request the activation key from your Heidelberg agency and enter it in "Activation Key".

Apply

The settings are saved and the upgrade is enabled when you click "Apply".

Cancel

Your last inputs are undone by clicking "Cancel" unless the "Apply" button was clicked.
