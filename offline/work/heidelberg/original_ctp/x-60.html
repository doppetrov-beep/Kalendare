Punches

This tab is used to configure the recorder punches. This is only possible in the "Service" or "Research and Development" mode, see Operating Mode.

Punch Mounting

The graphic shows the recorder with the locations where the punches are installed.

Cable Connection

This is where you must set the cable connections for the punches as they are installed and connected in the recorder.

Apply

Clicking "Apply" sends the settings to the recorder, and all calculations required for this punch configuration are performed and activated.

Cancel

Your last inputs are undone by clicking "Cancel" unless the "Apply" button was clicked.
