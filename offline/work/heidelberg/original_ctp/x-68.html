Remote Service

If servicing is necessary, the service support can access via remote service the parts of your computer that you unlock for them.

Note: If the default web address is not the right one, you can enter another one.

1. Click "Launch Internet Browser". The Internet browser displays showing a web page.

2. Fill out all the boxes of this form.

3. Click "Submit". This creates the online contact to the service support. In this dialog, the service support will now ask you to unlock certain parts of your computer that you can confirm or reject accordingly.

Caution: Even if requested to do so by your service support, you may not remove any parts of the casing from the device or use the key-operated switch. Opening of any parts of the casing not referred to in the operating manual can lead to considerable danger for the user.

Save

Click "Save" to save the address you entered so that it automatically appears after a restart.

Use Default

The default address appears in the "Address" box again when you click "Use Default". After a restart, the address saved with "Save" is displayed again.
