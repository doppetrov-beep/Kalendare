Material Wizard (8) Power Evaluation

In this tab, you can enter the values from the power test and calculate the new current power value.

Evaluation of the Power Test, see Evaluation of the Power Test for Known Plates

Material Name

This displays the name of the material that you used for the power test. The material name was taken from the "Material Wizard (3)" tab.

A

In this box, enter the number of the test strip that the power test determined as the correct one for the laser module.

Current power

This displays the currently applicable power value.

Calculate

This button is enabled if box A is filled out. Click "Calculate" to calculate the new current power value.

Apply

The settings are sent to the recorder and enabled when you click "Apply".

Close

Click "Close" to go back to "Material Wizard (1)".

Back

Click "Back" to go back to "Material Wizard (7)".

Continue

Click "Next" to open "Material Wizard (9)".
